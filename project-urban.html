Urban City

Inspired by the 90s editorial work of Chi Modu and Jonathan Mannion during the peak of the then emerging Hiphop scene.

Shot in the underground corridors of Rotterdam, this series plays with the contrast between raw urban architecture and a quiet, personal moment. Blending harsh black-and-white tones with warm ambient color, it captures the beauty hidden in concrete walls, dim lighting, and everyday city solitude.
